# Future Extension Modules Architecture

The Phase-1 POC provides abstract interfaces for expanding into full enterprise supply chain modules:

1. **Multi-Warehouse Inventory Optimizer** (`MultiWarehouseOptimizer`): Dynamic inventory rebalancing across hubs.
2. **Intelligent Procurement Optimizer** (`ProcurementOptimizer`): Automated bulk PO generation and multi-supplier lead time balancing.
3. **Supply Chain Digital Twin** (`NetworkDigitalTwin`): Graph representation of nodes, routes, and transit times.
4. **Route & Last-Mile Optimizer** (`RouteOptimizer`): Last-mile vehicle routing problem (VRP) solver.
5. **Retail Assortment & Space Optimizer** (`AssortmentOptimizer`): Shelf space allocation and planogram optimization.
6. **Customer 360 & Retail AI** (`Customer360Service`): Churn prediction and personalized reorder triggers.
7. **GeoAI Location Intelligence** (`GeoAIService`): Spatial demand heatmap analysis.
